Privacy policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026

This policy explains how Berrezueta Inc. ("we", "us") collects, uses and protects personal information when you visit our website, buy from our store or message us, including on WhatsApp.

Information we collect

  • Information you give us: your name, email, phone number, shipping address and the content of messages you send us.
  • Order information: the products you buy and your order history. Payments are handled by our payment processors; we do not store full card numbers.
  • Messaging information: when you message us on WhatsApp, we receive your phone number, your profile name and the messages you send, through the WhatsApp Business Platform operated by Meta.
  • Technical information: basic data such as browser type and pages visited, collected by our hosting and store providers.

How we use it

  • To process and deliver orders and answer your questions.
  • To reply to your WhatsApp messages. Some replies may be prepared with the help of automated and AI tools.
  • To run, secure and improve our services, and to meet legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal information.

Who we share it with

We share information only with providers that help us operate, and only as needed: our e-commerce platform, payment processors, shipping carriers, hosting providers, Meta (for WhatsApp messaging) and AI service providers that process message content on our behalf. We may also disclose information when required by law.

How long we keep it

We keep information only as long as needed for the purposes above or as required by law, then delete or anonymize it.

Your choices and rights

You can ask us to access, correct or delete your personal information, or stop messaging you, at any time. See data deletion or email berrezuetainc@gmail.com.

Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

Children

Our services are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.
